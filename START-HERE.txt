HOW CONVERSATIONS WORK
- Drafts -> edit -> Approve.
- You manually send that approved email outside the OS.
- Back in Drafts, click Mark as sent.
- The company appears in Conversations. Only the actual sent email is kept as
  the starting message; the OS does not put old draft/edit versions in your face.
- When they reply: paste subject + reply -> Save reply for AI.
- Conversation Manager reads the sent email + thread and creates a suggested reply.
- You edit/approve it and manually send it, then click Mark as sent.
- Close Conversation is always available manually.

AI COST CONTROL
- Conversation Manager makes ZERO AI calls when there is no pasted reply or due follow-up.
- It batches up to 5 reply/follow-up jobs into one routed AI request.
- It uses the existing OpenAI/Gemini Router switch. No Router code change is required.
- Iran first-contact drafts stay English-only by default to avoid spending extra output tokens.
